# Internal Compliance Playbook (Sample) — v1.0
SourceName: Internal Compliance Playbook
SourceLink: https://example.local/kb/compliance-playbook-v1
LastUpdated: 2026-02-09
 
## 1. Data breach notification
Policy ID: DP-SEC-001
Requirement:
- Vendor must notify Company of any Personal Data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware.
- Notification must include: nature of incident, affected data categories, approximate # of data subjects, mitigation steps, contact point.
Preferred contract language (fallback):
- "Vendor shall notify Company without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of a Security Incident involving Personal Data."
 
## 2. Subprocessors
Policy ID: DP-SEC-002
Requirement:
- Vendor must disclose subprocessors and provide a mechanism for Company to object to new subprocessors.
Preferred language:
- "Vendor shall maintain an up-to-date list of Subprocessors and provide at least thirty (30) days’ prior notice of any new Subprocessor. Company may object on reasonable grounds."
 
## 3. Data retention and deletion
Policy ID: DP-SEC-003
Requirement:
- Data retention period must be defined.
- Upon termination, Vendor must delete or return Personal Data within 30 days (unless legally required to retain).
Preferred language:
- "Upon termination, Vendor will delete or return Personal Data within thirty (30) days, unless retention is required by law."
 
## 4. Cross-border transfers
Policy ID: DP-PRIV-001
Requirement:
- If Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, Vendor must use an approved transfer mechanism (e.g., SCCs) and provide documentation upon request.
Preferred language:
- "Vendor shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA/UK except in accordance with applicable transfer mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses."
 
## 5. Audit rights
Policy ID: DP-GOV-001
Requirement:
- Company must have audit rights (direct or via third party) for compliance verification at least annually, subject to reasonable notice.
Preferred language:
- "Company may audit Vendor’s compliance once per year with reasonable notice."
 
## 6. Liability cap (data protection)
Policy ID: LEG-RISK-002
Guidance:
- Liability caps must not exclude liability for confidentiality breaches, IP infringement, or data protection obligations.
Preferred approach:
- Carve-outs for (a) confidentiality breach, (b) IP infringement, (c) data protection breach, (d) gross negligence/willful misconduct.
 
## 7. Governing law and jurisdiction
Policy ID: LEG-RISK-005
Guidance:
- Prefer governing law to align with Company contracting entity; avoid unusual venues that prevent enforcement.
 
## 8. Payment terms
Policy ID: FIN-STD-001
Guidance:
- Standard net terms: Net 30.
- Any late fee > 1.5% per month requires approval.
