MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
 
This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between

DataSpark Analytics Ltd. (“Vendor”) and Contoso Retail Pvt. Ltd. (“Company”).
 
1. Term

This Agreement begins on 1 January 2026 and continues for an initial term of

one (1) year, renewing automatically for successive one-year periods unless

terminated by either party.
 
2. Confidentiality

Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other.

Vendor’s confidentiality obligations shall survive indefinitely.
 
3. Data Processing

3.1 Vendor may process Personal Data solely to provide the Services.

3.2 Vendor may transfer Personal Data to any country worldwide without restriction.

3.3 Vendor shall notify Company of any Security Incident within thirty (30) days

after confirmation of the incident.

3.4 Vendor may engage subprocessors at its discretion and is not required to

notify Company of such subprocessors.
 
4. Data Retention

Vendor may retain Personal Data for as long as it determines necessary for

business purposes.
 
5. Deletion Upon Termination

Upon termination of this Agreement, Vendor will use commercially reasonable

efforts to delete Personal Data.
 
6. Audit Rights

Company shall have no right to audit Vendor’s systems, facilities, or compliance

practices.
 
7. Fees and Payment

Invoices are payable within ninety (90) days from receipt.

Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of three percent (3%) per month.
 
8. Limitation of Liability

Vendor’s total liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be

limited to the fees paid by Company in the one (1) month preceding the claim.

Vendor shall not be liable for any breach of confidentiality or data protection

obligations.
 
9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Ruritania, and the courts of

Ruritania shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
 
10. Order of Precedence

This Agreement shall prevail over any data processing addendum or privacy

terms.
 
END
